🩷 Meet DIE City

Welcome to DIE City! Most feminine nouns live here. Many nouns have special endings that help us predict DIE.

🏙️ DIE City Story

In DIE City lives a beautiful Familie. The Freiheit (-heit) and Schönheit (-keit) walk through the Straße. A Universität (-tät) welcomes a neue Möglichkeit (-keit). A Revolution (-ion) meets a Situation (-ion). A Musikschule (-schule) and Sprache (-sprache) help people learn. The Zeitung (-ung) brings Nachrichten. All these words belong to the pink DIE family. 🩷

🧠 Memory Tips

  • Almost all nouns ending with -ung are DIE.
  • Abstract ideas ending with -heit / -keit are DIE.
  • International words ending with -ion are usually DIE.
  • Many professions ending with -in are DIE.
