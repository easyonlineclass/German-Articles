📖 What are DER, DIE and DAS?

Every German noun has a grammatical gender. This means every noun belongs to one of three families:

🔵 DER

Masculine nouns

Example:
der Mann
the man

🩷 DIE

Feminine nouns

Example:
die Frau
the woman

💛 DAS

Neutral nouns

Example:
das Kind
the child

Why are articles important?

In English we usually say "the". In German we must know whether the noun is der, die or das.

🧠 Memory Idea

Think of German nouns as people living in three different cities:

  • 🔵 DER City
  • 🩷 DIE City
  • 💛 DAS City

Every noun has a home. Your job is to discover which city it belongs to.
